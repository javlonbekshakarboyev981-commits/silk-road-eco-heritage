# Silk Road Eco-Heritage: Save a Traditional Uzbek House

Official GitHub repository for the **Silk Road Eco-Heritage** initiative. This project is dedicated to restoring a traditional, zero-carbon Paxsa (mud-brick) family heritage house located in the historic city of Samarkand, Uzbekistan, and transforming it into a modern, solar-powered eco-hub.

## 🌟 Our Mission
We aim to preserve the architectural identity of our village heritage while implementing sustainable, green-energy solutions. By blending ancient building techniques with modern solar technology, this project serves as a live model for eco-friendly living and cultural preservation in the region.

## 🛠 Project Status & Transparency
This initiative is driven directly by our family on the ground. We believe in building in public and maintaining 100% financial and operational transparency. 

* **On-Chain Budget:** A highly detailed $80,000 budget breakdown is integrated directly into our official crowdsourcing profile.
* **Updates:** We document our restoration progress, from construction updates to solar energy grid integration, directly within our public campaign.
